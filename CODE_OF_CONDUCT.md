# Code of Conduct

## Standards

This project exists to measure harness contributions rigorously. We expect:

- **Technical honesty.** Report what you measured, not what you hoped to measure. Do not submit results you cannot reproduce. Do not omit trials that failed.
- **Good-faith participation.** Critique the measurement, not the person. Assume others are trying to get it right.
- **Respect for upstream work.** The corpus is built from commits other people wrote. Attribute them, link to them, and do not misrepresent what their code does.

## Scope

Benchmark design, measurement methodology, harness adapters, task selection, grading correctness, and the tooling that supports them are in scope. A harness's product decisions (pricing, UX, safety policies) are out of scope.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that misrepresent results, attribute claims incorrectly, or disregard this document after being reminded. Repeated violations may result in a permanent block from the repository.
